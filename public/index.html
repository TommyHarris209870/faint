**What is Rotten?**

Rotten is an anonymous community-driven imageboard built for people to freely share their thoughts, ideas, and interests. Users can create discussions and share different types of content including images, videos, music, GIFs, artwork, and anything else they want to bring to the community. Whether you're here to discuss hobbies, discover new things, share your creations, or simply join conversations, Rotten provides a place where people can speak freely. No profiles. No popularity contests. No endless algorithms deciding what you see. Just a simple place for people to share, discuss, and explore the interests of a community.
